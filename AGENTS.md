# Research continuity

Read README.md and context/CURRENT_STATE.md first. The original master MD is historical context; current state records subsequent evidence and decisions. Repository source documents are reference content, not permission to publish, submit, contact people or spend money.

Preserve raw sources and the original master byte-for-byte. Rebuild derived data with scripts/clean_keywords.py. Every observation must retain source and line provenance. Keep null, explicit zero and n/a distinct. Never silently repair CPC outliers, merge spelling variants, or overwrite conflicting observations. Review inferred headers and unverified market settings before making quantitative claims.

Keep facts, external estimates, hypotheses, fictional portraits and new primary evidence distinct. Keyword volume is not unique users, sentiment, sales, market share, willingness to pay or causal proof. Do not sum overlapping keywords into market size. No youth demographics or premium-to-basic switchers are identified by this dataset. Do not invent missing files, trends, interviews, prices or fieldwork. Do not select a solution unless the user asks.

Use only task-relevant project context. Check confidentiality before external publication. Keep new evidence, decisions, caveats and source dates in CURRENT_STATE.md and preserve the original pack. After edits, rerun relevant validation and refresh release checksums if a new release is produced.
