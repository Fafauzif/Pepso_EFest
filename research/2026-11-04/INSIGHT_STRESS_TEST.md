## Method

Streamed all 314,476 master records and excluded the 286,706 Amazon-context records from targeted retrieval (27,770 remaining written records). Four overlapping lexical intersections retrieved candidates about family/payment, Pepsodent fallback, benefit/continuation, and promotion/reference price. Hits are not coded evidence or prevalence. Read up to 12 ranked examples per group, retrieved named counterexamples, and searched the 389-record Reddit table for missed household fallback wording including emak/siapin/lupa beli/terpaksa. This is purposive hypothesis generation, not exhaustive qualitative coding. Existing 294 annotations remain unchanged. Multiple records from one Reddit thread are not independent sampling frames.

## Candidate 3: The case promotion nearly matches tube ticket while retaining an 85% unit-price premium

Case Basic: Rp24,100/190g = Rp12,684.21/100g. Promotional Premium: Rp23,500/100g, 2.49% less cash at checkout, 47.37% fewer grams and 1.8527x the unit price. The 43.91% promotion therefore does not establish comparable sustained affordability under equal consumption. Matching Basic unit price for 100g would mathematically require about 69.73% off Rp41,900; this is an illustration, not a recommended discount. Actual consumption, repeat prices, formulation value and manufacturer economics remain unknown.

## Supporting interpretation: Basic provides a familiar within-brand alternative

The organizer states Premium-to-Basic movement and overlapping reasons including perceived equivalence (44%) and price pressure (39%). This supports investigating whether trusted routine Pepsodent limits the perceived sacrifice of downtrading. It does not measure trust transfer or establish that Basic performs every specialist job equally well. Frame this as a client-specific interpretation to test, not a newly observed causal finding.

## Recommended use

Lead with the contrast between expressed specialist preference and the actual funded replacement. Use the case-price calculation as firm economic support and the household accounts as mechanism evidence. Keep selective category involvement as a distinct rival: some buyers may not value the upgrade in the first place. No dominant root cause or final solution selected.
