# Primary research kit — Pepsodent replenishment

Prepared 3 November 2026, Asia/Jakarta. **Planning tools only: no participants have been recruited or interviewed, no primary observations exist, and the CSV files contain headers only.** Bahasa Indonesia is used for fieldwork; this index and the rationale explain the design in English.

The purpose is to learn which explanations survive an account of a real replacement purchase. Public comments have made household payment, sensory fit, price, felt benefit and availability plausible, but have not established their prevalence or causal contribution to the case's Premium sales mix. Begin with actual purchases, then examine explanations. Do not ask respondents to agree that “premium is too expensive” or “discounts do not produce loyalty.”

## Adaptable fieldwork scope

Access to participants and cities is uncertain. Three teammates can use these versions without pretending convenience recruitment is representative:

| Version | Practical starting design | What it can do |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum | Two pilots, then 4–6 substantive interviews covering at least a switcher and a retained Premium contrast if accessible | Refine mechanisms, wording, recruitment, and missing evidence; report uncovered groups |
| Recommended first wave | Two pilots, then 8–12 substantive purchase-history interviews | Seek variation across buying paths and household authority; update after every 3–4 interviews |
| Expanded | Add interviews to address specific unresolved contrasts; invite consenting near-replenishment participants to one follow-up | Check whether explanations survive a later observed purchase and less convenient cases |

These are **logistical starting points, not saturation thresholds, quotas that must be filled with false eligibility, or sample sizes for population estimates**. Do not replace inaccessible switchers with generic toothpaste users and retain the original label. A pilot may be retained as substantive evidence only if eligibility, consent, guide version, and data quality support it; record the inclusion decision.

Seek several histories: (A) case-eligible Pepsodent Premium → Pepsodent Basic; (B) retained Pepsodent Premium; (C) Basic retained or a competitor path. Cross-cut each by self/family payer, own/shared tube, student/worker if accessible, sensitivity/whitening/general benefit, and living at home/away if the participant volunteers this information. These dimensions can overlap; an 18–30-year-old is not automatically an independent purchaser.

## Case time and SKU boundaries

The case horizon ends **30 June 2026**. Interviews conducted after that date provide current primary evidence; they do not automatically explain the earlier mix movement. Tag each purchase event as case-period, later, older, or uncertain. Retrospective accounts may cover the case period but remain subject to recall limitations. Never ask someone to invent an exact date or price.

Classify the actual variant against a **verified case SKU/tier roster**. Record pack name, size, image or receipt if voluntarily shared; do not infer Premium from a high paid price, product colour, “expert” wording, or the participant's use of “premium.” Keep uncertain tier classifications uncertain. This kit does not settle every SKU's case-tier membership.

## Evidence standard

A participant's sequence can be self-reported and credible without proving causation. Distinguish observed receipt/photo, exact quotation, participant's interpretation, analyst inference, and counterfactual stated preference. Self-reported sensitivity or cleanliness is not a clinical diagnosis/effectiveness test. A current intention to rebuy is not a completed repurchase; a promotion alongside repeat does not establish that the promotion caused repeat.

Record contradictions, missing alternatives, forgotten prices, third-party payer accounts, and rational satisfaction with Basic. Ask what would make the preferred explanation wrong. Do not score evidence strength by how dramatic a quotation sounds.

## Files and blank templates

All templates are CSV headers only, ready to copy to a new wave folder. See [DATA_DICTIONARY.md](DATA_DICTIONARY.md). Use participant codes such as your team's chosen code scheme; no fictional populated rows are included. Store contact details and raw identifying recordings outside the Git repository, with limited access and an agreed deletion date. Private Git history is still persistent history.

The minimum evidence packet for each completed interview is: consent status; screener and eligibility decision; guide version and interview date; purchase timeline with certainty/source fields; anonymized notes with exact quotations separated from interpretation; unresolved questions; and the hypothesis update with counterevidence.
