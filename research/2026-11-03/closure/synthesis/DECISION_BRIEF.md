# Decision brief: enough collection to begin discriminating primary research

3 November 2026, Asia/Jakarta. Private team use. No final strategy selected.

**The team can stop broad secondary collection.** The useful next step is to determine which mechanism explains actual replacement decisions among confirmed eligible people. The collection supports competing hypotheses and better questions; it does not identify the dominant cause of the Premium mix decline.

## What changes the diagnosis

**Replenishment is a funded household decision, not only an individual preference.** A TikTok commenter explicitly contrasts parent-purchased Sensodyne at home with personally salient kos pricing (`7552195449504547585`). Another account combines parental limits on changing toothpaste with reported sensory discomfort (`7425778994514150149`). Neither establishes age or Pepsodent Premium-to-Basic switching. The practical implication is to separate user, chooser, payer, shopper and co-users.

**Improvement can support continued use.** Tokopedia has an explicit Ultra White repurchase because the product fits (`1932700850`), plus Sensodyne benefit-linked commitment (`2145103290`) and long-use/repeat language (`2177591268`). These are rivals to an inevitable stop-after-relief story. The exact paid prices and target eligibility remain unknown.

**Promotion interest can coexist with loyalty.** Enzim reviewers describe long use and sensory fit while valuing bundles or requesting deals (`1469837237`, `2310809324`; the latter is post-case). Do not classify every promotion mention as weak loyalty or infer an effect of discounting from it.

**Exact-variant access remains plausible; blanket minimarket absence does not.** Official Indomaret regional offers advertised Sensitive Expert 100 g in June–July 2025. Current larger and smaller Premium packs also exist. No Jakarta branch checkout or physical shelf stock was verified. Test the desired SKU on the actual replacement route before proposing D2C or a new pack as the solution.

**Basic can be sufficient.** A buyer can understand Premium and afford it while assigning little value to its extra benefit. Include that person in research. The appropriate objective is durable value for suitable users, rather than assuming every Basic choice is an information failure.

## What the counts mean

The earlier written store retains 298,846 records/fingerprints, dominated by 286,706 international Amazon candidates across all retained product scopes. Those are not Indonesian young switchers. The new X exports contain 12,990 IDs; five earlier non-overlapping brand IDs bring the combined X platform total to 12,995. TikTok has 2,380 comments, 2,372 nonblank; 1,648 fall within 2022–H1 2026 using Asia/Jakarta. Tokopedia has 266 canonical reviews, including all 43 pilot reviews, with 263 nonempty texts and 256 written toothpaste candidates after the stated scope/quality exclusions.

The new annotation sets contain 294 closely reviewed records: X 109, TikTok 100, Tokopedia 85. Tokopedia's full 266 texts were also read for screening. These are documented random-calibration and purposive sets, not a representative prevalence sample. No verified target-age Premium-to-Basic sequence exists in the public corpus.

Source quality materially reduces independent information. X includes advertising, humour, unrelated matches and repeated templates. Eight TikTok videos returned 180 comments, consistent with the proposed cap; captions are unavailable. Tokopedia has 186 disagreements among 198 records with both date fields, plus 68 missing `reviewTime` values. Stars can concern delivery before use, and low ratings can concern packing. Shopee's failed pilot and 100 irrelevant UGREEN reviews are audit material, not toothpaste evidence.

## The decisions to resolve next

| Decision | Evidence needed |
|---|---|
| Is the barrier payment/authority or individual value? | Who actually chose and paid at the last replacement, with household and independent-purchase contrasts |
| Is the extra benefit durable and relevant? | Expected outcome, experienced change, evaluation period, sensory fit and the subsequent product |
| Is the intended variant accessible? | Exact SKU, outlet/date, alternatives, and a targeted shelf check only where warranted |
| Does a deal produce incremental sustained use? | First and next paid price, stock at home, pack quantity, prior usage and actual replacement |
| What does the mix decline represent? | Comparable-period value/volume/price/household bridge and a consistent tier definition |

The 27% to 20% movement is seven share points, not automatically the same percentage decline in absolute Premium revenue. A hypothetical comparable-period brand revenue index rising from 100 to 135 would keep Premium revenue at 27 when share reaches 20%; this is an arithmetic scenario, not actual company growth. Manufacturer contribution and incremental lift are unmeasured.

## Practical next wave

Use two pilot interviews, then an initial 8–12 purchase-history interviews, expanding when important contrasts remain unresolved. Prioritize confirmed 18–30 Premium-to-Basic switchers and include retained Premium, satisfied Basic, competitor choices, dependent/independent purchasing and shared/personal tubes. The counts are practical starting points, not proof of saturation.

Reconstruct the last two or three purchases: exact product, pack, chooser, payer, users, price actually paid, promotion, outlet, alternatives, what ran out and what happened afterward. Record uncertainty and use voluntarily available product/receipt prompts. A short follow-up near depletion can reveal later action. Refine any survey after the pilots; a convenience survey is not automatically representative.

Keep three strategy directions conditional: better specialist selection/expectations; household/funding design; dependable replenishment. Existing scanners, sampling, consultations, content and pack variants must be benchmarked before proposing equivalents. A useful test measures appropriate actual paid replacement and incremental contribution, with a prewritten reason to stop if the mechanism fails.
