# Competing hypothesis matrix

3 November 2026. This matrix records analyst assessments, not verified cause prevalence. Mechanism plausibility and applicability to confirmed 18–30 Pepsodent Premium-to-Basic switchers are separate. Sources are retained project artifacts.

## H01: User values Premium but a different payer or chooser selects Basic

**Mechanism:** Purchase authority and payment are separated from the specialist beneficiary.

**Prediction:** The user reports relevant benefit and preference; another household role controls the actual replacement and selects a lower-cost or shared option.

**Supporting evidence:** TikTok 7552195449504547585 contrasts parent-funded home use with kos price; TikTok 7425778994514150149 describes parental restrictions; Reddit jmz7mka describes spouse choice.

**Rival/counterevidence:** Reddit jn2d5tk describes shared specialist use supported by the mother's sensitivity; a payer may willingly fund Premium.

**What would weaken or falsify this account:** Eligible switchers control choice and payment, with no household constraint, yet still reject Premium for another reason.

**Primary test:** Reconstruct who noticed depletion, chose, ordered, paid and used the last two or three tubes; obtain a payer account only with permission.

**Mechanism assessment:** Plausible; direct public role-difference accounts. **Target applicability:** Unknown; no confirmed target switching sequence.

**Conditional direction:** Test household-relevant benefit or personal/shared tube logic only when the roles actually constrain purchase.

**Source paths:** ../tiktok/FINDINGS.md; ../../scale_reddit/findings.md

## H02: Independent purchasing changes the acceptable replacement outlay

**Mechanism:** A formerly supplied product becomes a personal budget decision; the relevant alternative and reference point change.

**Prediction:** Payer transition precedes the first lower-priced replacement despite known product fit.

**Supporting evidence:** TikTok 7552195449504547585 directly contrasts parent-funded use with self-salient kos pricing.

**Rival/counterevidence:** X 2015590249167274297 reports moving to a more expensive toothpaste after starting work; independence can support uptrading.

**What would weaken or falsify this account:** Payment responsibility and available budget did not change at replacement, or the person continued Premium after self-funding.

**Primary test:** Identify moving/working/payment transition dates and compare the last household-funded with first personally funded purchase.

**Mechanism assessment:** Plausible; one direct context contrast plus a strong rival. **Target applicability:** Unknown age, exact paid prices and next product in public accounts.

**Conditional direction:** Evaluate existing pack/cash-outlay access and appropriate targeting; do not assume all independent youth need cheaper products.

**Source paths:** ../tiktok/FINDINGS.md; ../x/FINDINGS.md

## H03: The desired exact variant is unavailable on the ordinary replenishment trip

**Mechanism:** A satisfied user cannot obtain the intended SKU and accepts a convenient default.

**Prediction:** The user seeks a specific variant, finds it absent/inconvenient, and selects Basic or another product among actual alternatives.

**Supporting evidence:** LZD-1574262094522499 combines fit, bundle savings and reported unavailability; Reddit jmz6rv4 describes difficulty finding a Closeup variant.

**Rival/counterevidence:** Official Indomaret 2025 Sensitive Expert 100 g promotions and current catalogues refute blanket channel absence.

**What would weaken or falsify this account:** Desired SKU was visible and purchasable on the actual trip, but the buyer rejected it for cost, need or experience.

**Primary test:** Reconstruct exact store/date/SKU/alternatives and follow with a narrow local shelf check where the account warrants it.

**Mechanism assessment:** Plausible exact-SKU mechanism; blanket minimarket absence weakened. **Target applicability:** No verified target stockout-to-Basic sequence or national stockout rate.

**Conditional direction:** Fix verified local replenishment execution before scaling demand creation.

**Source paths:** ../../retail/findings.md; ../../history_publicdata/promotion_events.json; ../../access_reviews/clean/lazada_reviews.json

## H04: The incremental benefit is not noticed or is judged by the wrong evaluation rule

**Mechanism:** Extra cost is compared with a benefit that is irrelevant, slow, mismatched, or difficult for the buyer to observe.

**Prediction:** The person expected a defined result, did not perceive it within their chosen period, and links that disappointment to the next purchase.

**Supporting evidence:** TAR-117079 reports no visible whitening despite favorable score; TikTok 7425822711702553350 describes perceived Pepsodent non-relief then Sensodyne relief.

**Rival/counterevidence:** TAR-161537 reports positive whitening; X 1600113707526524928 continues without visible change because comfort matters.

**What would weaken or falsify this account:** Eligible switchers experienced the desired outcome and understood its ongoing value, yet chose Basic for an unrelated constraint.

**Primary test:** Ask expected outcome, evaluation period, noticed changes, proof standard, exact variant, and replacement consequence without giving a desired benefit first.

**Mechanism assessment:** Plausible heterogeneous evaluation mechanisms. **Target applicability:** No representative benefit-failure rate or verified clinical comparison.

**Conditional direction:** Test appropriate selection and expectation-setting for the specific need; verify supported claims.

**Source paths:** ../../scale_reviews/findings.md; ../tiktok/FINDINGS.md; ../x/FINDINGS.md

## H05: Perceived symptom resolution removes the reason to keep paying more

**Mechanism:** The buyer interprets specialist use as episodic treatment and returns to routine care after improvement.

**Prediction:** Improvement precedes Basic replacement, with an explicit belief that the specialist job is finished.

**Supporting evidence:** LZD-1200640450822499 expresses an until-resolved expectation but does not observe later discontinuation.

**Rival/counterevidence:** Tokopedia 2145103290 links benefit to not wanting to switch;2177591268 reports long use and repeat; clinical episodic trial does not imply inevitable relapse.

**What would weaken or falsify this account:** Improvement is followed by sustained repeat or stopping precedes improvement and is explained by another constraint.

**Primary test:** Reconstruct before/during/after symptom course, exact next product and reasoning; keep clinical guidance separate from commercial interviewing.

**Mechanism assessment:** Testable but weak direct evidence for actual stop-after-relief sequence. **Target applicability:** Unverified; no authenticated target sequence.

**Conditional direction:** Clarify appropriate ongoing value only if the endpoint interpretation is demonstrated; do not invent dependence or fear.

**Source paths:** ../../access_reviews/clean/lazada_reviews.json; ../tokopedia/FINDINGS.md; ../../literature/findings.md

## H06: Sensory discomfort or poor fit outweighs the specialist promise

**Mechanism:** Taste, foam, dryness, irritation, or mouthfeel makes continued use unattractive.

**Prediction:** A specific experienced sensation precedes and is explicitly connected to replacement.

**Supporting evidence:** TikTok 7425778994514150149 reports dark Pepsodent dryness;7581114937188221704 reports whitening-use discomfort then ordinary-product use; Enzim 1469837237 values low foam.

**Rival/counterevidence:** Tokopedia 1932700850 reports Ultra White repurchase because cocok; different users prefer different sensory profiles.

**What would weaken or falsify this account:** The user likes the experience and reports no adverse sensation, while the actual replacement is driven by payer, price or access.

**Primary test:** Ask spontaneous descriptions, timing, comparison, concurrent changes and link to purchase; do not diagnose ingredients or clinical harm.

**Mechanism assessment:** Direct reported experiences; heterogeneous and self-attributed. **Target applicability:** Unknown exact variants/ages in several accounts; no clinical causality.

**Conditional direction:** Address verified need/experience fit and supported expectations, rather than assuming more foam or stronger mint is universally better.

**Source paths:** ../tiktok/FINDINGS.md; ../tokopedia/FINDINGS.md; ../../literature/findings.md

## H07: Variant comparison is difficult enough to trigger a familiar default

**Mechanism:** Need uncertainty and comparison effort make Basic an acceptable low-effort choice.

**Prediction:** The shopper struggles to choose a suitable Premium variant before rejecting price and defaults despite relevant need.

**Supporting evidence:** Portfolio complexity and conditional choice-overload literature make this worth testing; no observed target choice task yet.

**Rival/counterevidence:** Experienced shoppers may benefit from variety; easy selection followed by price rejection is not overload.

**What would weaken or falsify this account:** Participants identify the suitable variant confidently and quickly but reject it on economics or experience.

**Primary test:** Observe an actual need-based SKU comparison before offering guidance; record reasoning, uncertainty, fallback and effect of clarification.

**Mechanism assessment:** Conditional theoretical plausibility; direct local decision evidence limited. **Target applicability:** Unknown; product count is not a behavioural measure.

**Conditional direction:** Improve a demonstrated decision difficulty using existing tools; avoid an undifferentiated new quiz.

**Source paths:** ../../literature/findings.md; ../../brand_campaigns/findings.md

## H08: Discounted trial is not repeated at the subsequent price

**Mechanism:** Trial economics differ from replacement economics through cash constraint, reference price or weak underlying need.

**Prediction:** The same buyer tries at a deal, then knowingly rejects an actual later price and selects Basic, controlling for stock and need.

**Supporting evidence:** Deep case/example offers and requests for more discounts establish offer architecture and promotion interest, not the complete mechanism.

**Rival/counterevidence:** Enzim 1469837237 and2310809324 combine long use/fit with deal language; promotion can subsidize loyalty or accelerate timing.

**What would weaken or falsify this account:** Deal users later repeat at ordinary prices, or apparent nonrepeat is explained by remaining stock, larger packs or unrelated change.

**Primary test:** Obtain first and next paid prices, pack quantity, baseline use, stock at home and next actual purchase; compare like cohorts.

**Mechanism assessment:** Plausible; causal direction and magnitude unidentified. **Target applicability:** No regular-price target retention cohort.

**Conditional direction:** Test sustained funded repeat and incrementality rather than optimizing trial volume alone.

**Source paths:** ../../history_publicdata/promotion_events.json; ../tokopedia/FINDINGS.md; ../../literature/findings.md

## H09: A personal specialist need is funded through a shared household tube

**Mechanism:** The incremental cost is spread across users with unequal needs and a faster shared depletion cycle.

**Prediction:** Only one person values the specialist benefit but multiple users consume it, and household replacement reverts to a general tube.

**Supporting evidence:** Household accounts establish shared products and unequal preferences; case pack economics makes the interaction possible.

**Rival/counterevidence:** Reddit jn2d5tk and Tokopedia family-use accounts show shared specialist needs can justify household Premium.

**What would weaken or falsify this account:** The tube is personal or several users value the specialist outcome, and sharing does not influence replacement.

**Primary test:** Map each user, need, tube, consumption arrangement and chooser; compare shared versus separate replenishment logic.

**Mechanism assessment:** Plausible household mechanism, not measured prevalence. **Target applicability:** No verified target household cost allocation or dose data.

**Conditional direction:** Evaluate personal/shared product logic when practical and appropriately valued; do not assume sharing always harms Premium.

**Source paths:** ../../synthesis/SCALE_SYNTHESIS.md; ../tokopedia/FINDINGS.md; ../../synthesis/case_household_cost_scenarios.csv

## H10: Basic is a rational sufficient final choice for some buyers

**Mechanism:** The added specialist outcome has little incremental value relative to accepted routine care and recurring cost.

**Prediction:** The buyer can afford, find and understand Premium but prefers Basic because it already meets the actual job.

**Supporting evidence:** Case unit-price difference and varied evaluation rules make sufficiency a required rival; evidence does not establish clinical equivalence.

**Rival/counterevidence:** Explicit benefit-driven repeat shows some needs sustain specialist value; a stated lack of need can also conceal misfit or low knowledge.

**What would weaken or falsify this account:** An appropriately matched, experienced benefit changes the actual funded replacement, showing previously unrecognized value.

**Primary test:** Recruit satisfied Basic users and ask what success is, what would justify more, and what they would lose by changing; avoid shaming.

**Mechanism assessment:** Essential rational-choice rival; direct target applicability needs testing. **Target applicability:** Unknown addressable share; not every Basic buyer is a suitable Premium target.

**Conditional direction:** Target appropriate durable needs and accept nonaddressable contexts rather than forcing universal uptrading.

**Source paths:** ../../PEPSODENT_DEEP_RESEARCH.md; ../tokopedia/FINDINGS.md; ../../literature/findings.md

## H11: Part of the observed mix change reflects accounting or purchase composition

**Mechanism:** Denominator growth, realized price, pack size, channel mix, timing or tier definitions change reported share without equivalent individual abandonment.

**Prediction:** Mix changes differ from absolute Premium volume/revenue/household repeat after comparable-period and SKU adjustments.

**Supporting evidence:** Case discloses share movement; arithmetic admits several absolute outcomes; public corpus does not supply the needed transaction bridge.

**Rival/counterevidence:** Organizer explicitly states downtrading; true individual Premium-to-Basic replacement may still contribute materially.

**What would weaken or falsify this account:** Consistent longitudinal data show equivalent decline among comparable eligible buyers after controlling mix, timing and measurement.

**Primary test:** Seek organizer/company clarification on definitions; in interviews distinguish one Basic purchase, concurrent tubes and complete abandonment.

**Mechanism assessment:** Arithmetic possibility; actual contribution unmeasured. **Target applicability:** No private transaction or cohort data.

**Conditional direction:** Define the business metric and magnitude before choosing reach, repeat or price objectives.
