# Pepsodent Premium at replenishment: diagnosis, competing explanations, and the next useful evidence

Final-session synthesis, 3 November 2026, Asia/Jakarta. Written for the private E-Fest team. This report integrates the earlier public-source research with the user's X, TikTok, and Tokopedia exports. It is secondary analysis, not completed team fieldwork. No final strategy is selected.

## 1. The conclusion to carry into the next chat

The question has become more precise: **under what conditions does an extra product benefit remain worth choosing, paying for, and finding when the tube runs out?** Premium trial and sustainable Premium replenishment are different outcomes. The same person can like the product, accept its benefit, and still replace it with something else because a different payer, store, price, household requirement, or immediate need governs the next purchase.

The evidence now supports a set of plausible mechanisms and several useful counterexamples. It does not identify which mechanism explains most of the case's decline. The most defensible working diagnosis is that some specialist benefits may fail to become a durable, funded choice at replenishment, even while Pepsodent remains a trusted routine brand. This is a hypothesis about the decision process, not a measured brand-perception result or an estimate of switching prevalence.

Five findings should change the team's thinking:

1. **Household dependence can operate through money, authority, taste, or shared consumption.** A TikTok commenter contrasts using parent-funded Sensodyne at home with facing its price when living independently. Another describes parental restrictions on changing toothpaste. These are different mechanisms and need different questions. Neither establishes the commenter's age or a Pepsodent Premium-to-Basic sequence.
2. **Experienced benefit can sustain Premium.** The new Tokopedia data include an explicit Pepsodent Ultra White repurchase and a Sensodyne account connecting experienced benefit with not wanting to switch. These challenge any universal story that Premium is inevitably a one-off or that symptom improvement necessarily leads to abandonment.
3. **A loyal user can still seek a promotion.** Enzim accounts combine long use or sensory fit with bundle discounts and requests for more promotions. Promotion language alone cannot distinguish fragile deal dependence from an established user making a sensible purchase.
4. **Availability needs a narrower definition.** Official historical Indomaret promotions contradict blanket claims that Pepsodent Premium is absent from minimarkets. Exact-variant, branch, timing, visibility, and shopping-route failures remain plausible.
5. **The bottleneck is now explanation quality.** The corpus is large enough to design discriminating primary questions. More generic social posts or five-star delivery reviews would not establish target eligibility, actual paid prices, the next replacement, or the counterfactual purchase.

The team should therefore move into targeted purchase-history interviews, preserving rival explanations and a route for Basic to be the rational answer. The purpose of primary research is to discover what actually happened and why, not to obtain agreeable quotations for a campaign already chosen.

## 3. Diagnose the metric before diagnosing the consumer

The case reports Premium's share of Pepsodent sales moving from 27% in 2022 to 20% in H1 2026. The difference is seven percentage points, and the share itself falls by about 25.9% relative to its starting level. This is not automatically a 25.9% fall in Premium revenue, volume, purchasers, repeat, or contribution. The annual and half-year labels also warn against treating raw period totals as comparable.

For a revenue-share measure, Premium revenue is total comparable Pepsodent revenue multiplied by Premium's share. A simple index illustrates the denominator problem:

| Hypothetical comparable-period total revenue | Starting Premium at 27% | Ending total | Ending Premium at 20% | Change in absolute Premium revenue |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Starting total fixed at 100 | 27 | 100 | 20 | −25.9% |
| Starting total fixed at 100 | 27 | 120 | 24 | −11.1% |
| Starting total fixed at 100 | 27 | 135 | 27 | 0% |
| Starting total fixed at 100 | 27 | 150 | 30 | +11.1% |

These are arithmetic scenarios under a revenue-share interpretation and comparable period exposure, not observations about actual Pepsodent revenue. They show why the metric definition matters. Respect the organizer's stated downtrading premise while keeping the strength of inference proportional to the disclosed data.

A useful revenue bridge is purchasing households × purchase occasions per household × grams per occasion × realized manufacturer revenue per gram. Each component can change independently. A larger pack can lengthen the replenishment interval without reducing usage. A discounted bundle can move purchases forward. A household can purchase Basic for one user and Premium for another. One Basic transaction is therefore insufficient to classify complete Premium abandonment.

The case's 38% Basic and 55% Premium margin illustration permits another conditional teaching calculation. If shares are revenue shares, the business is represented by only these tiers, and tier margins remain fixed, a 27% Premium mix gives 42.59% blended margin; 20% gives 41.40%, a 1.19-point change. This is not a reconstruction of actual company margin. Shelf prices are not manufacturer realization; corporate financial figures cover other products and accounting scopes. Do not multiply the case's retail price by the margin and present the result as actual Unilever profit. [Case and calculations: ../../synthesis/case_economics.json; ../../../../sources/transcripts/Case_Study_Transcript.md]

The internal-data request, if access ever becomes possible, should specify tier definitions, comparable time windows, value and volume by SKU/channel, paid and realized prices, promotion funding, household penetration, purchase frequency, pack size, inventory effects, and contribution. Without these, the team can propose mechanisms and tests but cannot allocate seven share points to them.

## 4. Follow the replenishment decision rather than an advertising funnel alone

The useful unit is the replacement occasion. Reconstruct it in stages:

| Stage | Question | Failure that can look like weak Premium demand |
|---|---|---|
| Need | What problem was the user trying to solve? | No relevant specialist need or a change in need |
| Choice | Which benefit, variant and pack seemed appropriate? | Misfit, uncertain claims, low attention, or deliberate rejection |
| First funding | Who authorized and paid for trial? | A subsidized or household-funded first purchase is mistaken for personal willingness to pay |
| Experience | What was noticed, and what counted as success? | Benefit not noticed, judged too early, adverse sensation, wrong evaluation rule |
| Depletion | Who noticed the tube running out, and what else was at home? | Shared inventory or stockpiling alters the observed purchase interval |
| Shopping | Where was the replacement bought; what was available? | Exact variant absent, inconvenient outlet, or an alternative already supplied |
| Payment | What was actually paid, by whom, for how much? | Recurring cash constraint, deal anchor, household allocation, minimum basket |
| Afterward | What was used next, by whom, and for how long? | Trial, substitution, concurrent use and abandonment are confused |

This structure prevents awareness from becoming the explanation for every failure. A person can recognize Premium, choose the right variant and feel a benefit, yet be unable to fund the next tube. Another can afford and find it but reasonably prefer Basic. Those two people should not receive the same intervention by default.

For interviews, begin with the last purchase and work backward. Ask about specific product names, package appearance, actual payer, approximate dates, price certainty and alternatives before asking general opinions. The [primary-research kit](../primary_research/) supplies practical instruments. Receipts or product photographs, where voluntarily available, can improve reconstruction but do not eliminate recall or selection error.

## 5. Household economics: user, chooser and payer are distinct

TikTok comment `7552195449504547585`, dated 20 September 2025, says: “sensodyne mahall, kalo di rumah bonyok pd pake jdinya ngikut dibeliin, tp kl dikosan 50k buat odol hellow?? 😭”. The speaker describes a cost becoming personally salient when living separately. The implication is not that Premium suddenly became worse. The relevant alternative changed from a household-supplied tube to a self-funded purchase. [Raw TikTok file, zero-based row 1861; video 7551727897510284562; see platform evidence tables.]

This account strengthens the plausibility of a payer-transition mechanism. It does not identify the speaker's age, exact Sensodyne pack, actual paid amount, subsequent purchase, or Pepsodent switching. The phrase “50k” should not be entered as a verified transaction price. It is a stated price reference.

Comment `7425778994514150149`, 14 October 2024, combines parental restrictions on changing brands with a reported dry/acid sensation after a dark Pepsodent variant. Household authority and sensory experience coexist. The exact product is not established by “yg hitam,” and the account cannot diagnose an ingredient or clinical harm. It is still useful because asking only “Who paid?” would miss a family rule about changing products. [Raw TikTok row 2216.]

The earlier Reddit records add important rivals. `jmz7mka` describes a spouse choosing brands and preferring a taste the speaker dislikes. `jn2d5tk` describes shared Systema partly because the mother finds it comfortable for sensitivity. Shared consumption can support specialization when several users value it. A household is not necessarily an obstacle to Premium. [../../scale_reddit/findings.md; ../../synthesis/SCALE_SYNTHESIS.md]

X adds a consequential life-stage rival. Post `2015590249167274297`, 26 January 2026, reports starting work, moving from a censored Pepsodent name to a more expensive toothpaste, and experiencing fewer mouth ulcers. Product identity, age and clinical cause are unverified. It nevertheless challenges an assumption that financial independence necessarily pushes people downward: new income can enable an individually preferred upgrade. Post `1927222520979058832`, 27 May 2025, retrospectively interprets a mother's childhood rejection of Closeup in favour of Ritadent as a preference for a cheaper option. That memory illustrates possible household price authority, not a current observed target decision or a verified explanation from the mother. [../x/evidence.jsonl]

Separate at least six roles: user, beneficiary, chooser, payer, shopper/order placer, and person managing depletion. One person can hold all six; a household can distribute them. A student may choose but not pay. A parent may pay but delegate choice. A person may buy a specialist tube for a relative without using it. Reviews written on behalf of someone else are not evidence that the writer had the reported condition.

Three different household mechanisms require different tests:

- **Payment transition:** the user begins paying personally. Compare the first self-funded replacement with earlier household-funded purchases.
- **Preference/authority mismatch:** the payer or chooser rejects a product that another user values. Reconstruct who made the final decision and why.
- **Shared-benefit economics:** only one person needs a specialist benefit while several consume the tube, or several users share the need and support the cost. Record who uses which tube rather than assuming one household equals one toothpaste.

Calling all three “switching cost” loses precision. Beginning independent purchasing may reveal a new out-of-pocket cost and reference point. Conventional switching costs—search, learning, exit penalties, benefits lost—are separate possibilities. The intervention must follow the observed mechanism.

## 6. Affordability has a checkout dimension and a sustained-use dimension

The case compares Basic 190 g at Rp24,100 with Sensitive Expert 100 g at Rp41,900, plus a Rp23,500 promotional Premium example. At these case prices, Basic is about Rp12,684 per 100 g; regular Premium is 3.30 times that unit price, and promotional Premium is still 1.85 times it. The promotional tube is slightly cheaper at checkout than Basic while containing substantially fewer grams. A shopper can afford that trial and still find replenishment more expensive over time.

| Case example | Tube ticket | Grams | Price per 100 g |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic | Rp24,100 | 190 | Rp12,684 |
| Sensitive Expert regular | Rp41,900 | 100 | Rp41,900 |
| Sensitive Expert promotional example | Rp23,500 | 100 | Rp23,500 |

Tube duration cannot be inferred from grams alone. It depends on users, frequency, actual quantity dispensed and wastage. For a transparent illustration only, assume two uses per day and 1 g per use per person. This is not dosing advice or measured behaviour. One user would consume 60 g in 30 days, giving modeled monthly cost of about Rp7,611 for Basic, Rp25,140 for regular Premium and Rp14,100 for promotional Premium. Four users would consume 240 g, giving about Rp30,442, Rp100,560 and Rp56,400 respectively. The ratios persist under equal assumed consumption, but actual usage and shared-tube allocation can differ. [../../synthesis/case_household_cost_scenarios.csv]

Now distinguish four economic questions. Is the next cash payment affordable? Is the cost over the period acceptable? Is the incremental benefit worth that cost? Is the price understood as fair relative to the shopper's reference? Someone may answer yes to any three and no to the fourth. Coding every mention of “mahal” as one cause obscures the decision.

Pack interpretation is equally conditional. A small pack can lower trial risk, signal concentration or quality, feel poor value, or run out inconveniently. The Tokopedia Enzim review `2191122942`, dated 8 July 2026, expected a larger pack but received two small ones. This is current post-case context and an expectation example, not proof that smaller Premium packs cause the case decline. Review `2105728218`, 19 May 2026, objects to a minimum purchase of three while wanting to try other flavours. The relevant friction is basket architecture and variety access, not simply unit price. [../tokopedia/FINDINGS.md]

The current retail data already show 100 g and 160 g Sensitive Expert; a 60 g minimarket catalogue listing also exists. An idea to introduce a larger or smaller pack must therefore identify the actual channel, need or execution gap. Compare a known pack in a known store and time before claiming innovation. [../../retail/findings.md, records WATAPI-PEPSODENT-005 and -031]

## 7. Promotions can recruit trial, subsidize loyalty, or move timing

The new Tokopedia evidence directly challenges the binary “loyal user versus promotion buyer.” Enzim review `1469837237`, dated 14 May 2024, describes longstanding use, discovery of a TikTok bundle promotion, pleasant low-foam experience, and a cheaper purchase. Review `2310809324`, 19 September 2026, describes continued Enzim use because other toothpaste feels unpleasant and requests more promotions. The latter is after the case endpoint. Both accounts can be truthful without establishing regular-price retention, promotion dependence, or a causal effect of the deal. [../tokopedia/FINDINGS.md; exact source rows in evidence tables]

The earlier literature provides a reason to compare cohorts. Neslin and Shoemaker's model offers a composition explanation for lower repeat after promotions: deals can recruit people with lower baseline probability of choosing the brand. Anderson and Simester's catalogue field experiments provide a counterexample to universal discount pessimism: effects differed between new and established customers. Neither supplies a toothpaste effect size. [LIT003–004, ../../literature/clean/reviewed_evidence.csv]

Four promotional mechanisms should be kept separate:

1. A deal makes a genuinely suitable product affordable enough for first trial and later repeated use.
2. A deal recruits a shopper with weak underlying need or willingness to pay, who would not otherwise choose it.
3. A deal subsidizes a buyer who would have purchased the same product anyway.
4. A deal brings forward an existing purchase or increases stock at home, reducing apparent later purchases without reducing usage.

Public review prose rarely distinguishes all four. A campaign-linked “will buy again” is weaker evidence than an explicit past repurchase, and neither gives actual manufacturer contribution. A label “special promo” on today's listing does not establish the price paid when an older reviewer bought.

For primary work, reconstruct first and next paid prices, pack quantity, prior brand use, need, time to depletion, stock at home and the next actual choice. A later test of promotions needs comparable cohorts and an appropriate counterfactual. No elasticity, retention curve, incremental lift, or sustainable demand quantity can be estimated from these accessible comments alone.

## 8. Experience: “clean,” “works,” and “worth it” are different evaluation rules

The notes asked what clean or impact feels like. The evidence shows why the question matters. Immediate mint, foam, smoothness, dryness and taste can be the user's evaluation rule even when the product is sold for sensitivity, gum care or gradual appearance change. A buyer can like the sensation without noticing the promoted benefit, or dislike a sensation despite a benefit.

The earlier TryandReview examples show a favorable score with no visible whitening after more than a month (`TAR-117079`), sensory/pack appreciation without close attention to whitening (`TAR-160280`), a positive two-week experience (`TAR-161537`), and reported repurchases despite modest visible change (`TAR-254032`, with incentive caveats). These are subjective and source-selected, but they prevent a universal “whitening never works” or “whitening result is the only source of value” story. [../../scale_reviews/findings.md; ../../synthesis/SCALE_SYNTHESIS.md]

X post `1600113707526524928`, 6 December 2022, reports months without an apparent visible difference but continued use because the product feels comfortable and does not coincide with mouth ulcers. Its parent product context is unavailable, so it cannot become a Pepsodent performance claim. Post `1540355497089728512`, 24 June 2022, describes perceived whitening, replacing the product when it ran out, seeing colour return, and then using the earlier product again. The exact SKU is unidentified. This is the sort of sequence that a one-time review would miss: substitution need not be permanent abandonment, and sensory preference can coexist with perceived appearance value. [../x/evidence.jsonl]

Tokopedia Ultra White review `1932700850`, dated 18 February 2026, explicitly says “beli lagi krn cocok.” This is a stated repurchase tied to fit. It does not tell us the actual paid price, number of previous purchases, target age, or the specific quality meant by cocok. It is nevertheless stronger evidence of past repeated purchase than generic approval.

TikTok `7425822711702553350`, dated 15 October 2024, describes Pepsodent not helping perceived sensitivity and improvement after returning to Sensodyne. Exact Pepsodent variant, diagnosis and regimen are unknown. It supports investigating need-fit and observed alternatives; it does not establish comparative clinical efficacy. `7581114937188221704`, dated 7 December 2025, describes discomfort during whitening use and changing to an ordinary product. Brand and exact category are insufficient to classify it as a verified Pepsodent Premium-to-Basic event. [Raw TikTok rows 681 and 2012; ../tiktok/FINDINGS.md]

The relevant clinical literature must remain separate. The retained Sälzer trial compared formulations over eight weeks and found sensory ratings differed while measured plaque/gingival outcomes did not differ significantly between those formulations. Multiple ingredients differed; funding and population transfer matter. It does not prove every toothpaste equivalent, that foam indicates cleaning, or that SLS explains any particular commenter's experience. [LIT013; ../../literature/findings.md]

Ask the sequence: what outcome was expected; what was noticed; how quickly; what counted as proof; what else changed; and whether that influenced the next purchase. Avoid telling a respondent which sensation should be valued. Appropriate product claims require relevant formulation evidence, not a compilation of agreeable reviews.

## 9. Improvement does not have one inevitable consequence

The initial hypothesis that a sensitivity user stops after feeling better is useful, but needs a rival. Tokopedia Sensodyne review `2145103290`, dated 10 June 2026, explicitly links having experienced benefit to not wanting to switch. Review `2177591268`, 30 June 2026, describes long use, benefit and repeat order. Those accounts illustrate benefit as a reason to continue. They are not Pepsodent retention measures. [../tokopedia/FINDINGS.md]

The earlier Lazada account `LZD-1200640450822499`, 1 July 2023, expresses an expectation of continuing until discomfort resolves. It does not observe subsequent discontinuation. Together these records support two rival interpretations: relief can remove the perceived reason to pay, or relief can establish why the product is worth continuing. The interview must determine which happened in the actual sequence.

The retained exploratory episodic-use trial does not justify an inevitable relapse narrative either. Its active-treatment improvement and power limitations matter, and it did not detect a simple universal between-regimen difference. [LIT024; ../../literature/findings.md] Do not use commercial objectives to invent medical dependence or pressure people to continue a formulation irrespective of need. Investigate their interpretation and seek appropriate professional context for any care recommendation.

## 10. Availability, marketing and choice: narrow the claim before proposing the solution

The official Indomaret regional flyer lists Sensitive Expert Original/Whitening 100 g at Rp19,900 against Rp39,900 for 26 June–9 July 2025. This is evidence of a specified historical offer. It is not proof every branch had stock, but it is enough to reject the blanket conclusion that Premium was absent from ordinary minimarket channels. [../../history_publicdata/promotion_events.json; ../../PEPSODENT_DEEP_RESEARCH.md, section 4]

Public catalogue existence, a stock-status field, local purchasability, an observed shelf and national weighted distribution are different measures. The Watsons API can return stock-like flags with unresolved purchasability/loading context; no Jakarta branch or checkout was verified. The useful next test asks whether a named user sought a named variant at a named replacement occasion and what alternatives they faced.

The earlier satisfied Lazada account `LZD-1574262094522499` combines bundle savings, product fit and reported availability difficulty. The listing title may have changed over time. Treat it as a clue that value and access can coexist, not proof of a Pepsodent stockout rate or actual Basic fallback. A Reddit report of difficulty finding a Closeup variant is a category comparator, not Pepsodent distribution evidence.

D2C dominance cannot be inferred from supposed retail reluctance. Separate brand-owned checkout, brand-branded marketplace storefront, retailer, independent seller and demand-generation partner. A label “official” does not resolve operator, inventory, invoicing or customer-data economics. The collected materials establish a communication and retail/marketplace ecosystem, not channel sales shares.

The brand audit also shows existing specialist efforts. Ultra White has a documented fashion/beauty route, including the JFW 2026 edition event held in October 2025. Expert-related materials use health and professional-support framing. ShadeScan AI, self-checks, science explanation, consultation-linked programs and sampling already exist. A proposal for an AI quiz, content, KOL endorsement, consultation or sample must demonstrate the specific missing mechanism it improves. Existence is not effectiveness; novelty alone is not the business test. [../../brand_campaigns/findings.md]

Choice overload likewise remains conditional. The retained meta-analyses identify heterogeneity and moderators rather than a universal more-options penalty. A shopper might understand every option but reject price; understand the need but be unable to compare; or know the preferred SKU and fail to find it. Only the middle case is primarily a selection task. A quiz cannot repair all three. [LIT001–002; ../../literature/findings.md]

The brand-meaning question needs disciplined language. Based on brand material, an intended-positioning synthesis is accessible, trusted household tooth care and confident smiles, with specialist offers for particular needs. That sentence is an analyst interpretation of communications, not a measured mental association shared by Indonesians. In primary work, first ask unprompted associations, then explore what Premium adds or conflicts with. Do not give the desired positioning before measuring the response.

## 11. Basic may be sufficient, and that changes the objective

A person choosing Basic can be informed, able to afford Premium, and satisfied with the choice. General prevention, familiarity, accepted taste, family compatibility and a lower recurring cost may meet their actual job. This is not evidence that every Basic product matches every specialist formulation clinically; it is a recognition that the commercial upgrade must create relevant incremental value for that person.

Interview such people deliberately. Ask what Basic achieves, what they would lose by changing, and what extra outcome would matter. If the answer is no relevant extra need or no valued improvement, generic education may simply add cost without a durable reason to upgrade. If the need exists but benefit is misunderstood, selection/expectation support becomes more plausible. If the need is understood and met but price dominates, affordability or targeting becomes the issue.

The case calls for restoring Premium participation, but a credible solution should specify who is economically and appropriately addressable. Do not assume that moving every satisfied Basic household upward is feasible, desirable, or socially beneficial. Specialist fit and access to appropriate care are better social-outcome candidates than Premium mix itself.

## 13. Prioritize hypotheses by what evidence would discriminate them

The [hypothesis matrix](HYPOTHESIS_MATRIX.md) records eleven explanations, expected observations, support, rivals, falsifiers and conditional implications. It separates mechanism plausibility from applicability to the target. Public text can make a mechanism credible while leaving its frequency among eligible switchers unknown.

Begin primary work with three high-consequence contrasts:

**Payment/household versus individual persuasion.** If a person likes and understands the product but cannot influence or fund replacement, more individual awareness is insufficient. If the person controls and pays but rejects the experience, a household message is poorly targeted.

**Benefit/experience versus pure affordability.** If regular-price rejection follows weak or adverse experience, lower price may only subsidize misfit. If experienced benefit is strong and loss aversion around symptoms supports repeat, a sustained-value route may be viable. If the benefit matters but cash flow prevents purchase, the economics differ again.

**Exact-SKU access versus rational fallback.** If people repeatedly seek the desired variant and cannot obtain it on the ordinary trip, an execution route may matter. If it is on the shelf and known but Basic meets their need at preferred cost, availability is not the explanation for that occasion.

These are priorities for learning, not a ranking of proven causes. Include a diagnostic check on the metric in parallel whenever company/organizer clarification becomes available.

## 14. Conditional strategy directions and tests

Three broad directions remain open. They can combine only after the team identifies which mechanism matters for whom.

| Direction | Evidence trigger | Candidate action space | Test and stopping rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Make specialist value understandable and observable | Relevant need but incorrect selection, mismatched expectations or evaluation rule | Clear benefit/variant comparison and appropriate expectations using existing brand/professional tools | Better appropriate selection and actual paid replacement; stop if understanding improves without decision change because cost or access dominates |
| Design around funding and household decisions | User values product but payer, shared consumption or independent purchase transition changes replacement | Household-relevant communication, personal versus shared tube logic, accessible existing pack/basket options | More appropriate funded repeat with acceptable contribution; stop if household roles do not affect actual choice or unit economics fail |
| Make replenishment dependable in the ordinary route | Desired exact SKU sought but absent/inconvenient at replacement | Correct variant visibility, channel handoff, replenishment reminders or fulfilment execution where justified | Higher successful intended replacement and incremental contribution; stop if product was already readily available and rejected |

Avoid turning these into fixed concepts prematurely. A shopper can rationally belong outside an economically reachable premium-return segment. Do not automatically add a scanner, subscription, trial pack, app or KOL because it is fashionable. First specify the decision changed, why current execution does not do it, what operational condition must hold, and what would make the team stop.

Evaluate incrementality at the correct level. A Pepsodent gain from another Unilever oral-care brand may be largely portfolio transfer. A Premium sale replacing Basic needs the displaced Basic contribution included. A coupon to an already loyal buyer may lower realization without changing choice. Stockpiling shifts timing. A financial scenario should use incremental manufacturer revenue, relevant variable and channel costs, displaced baseline contribution and intervention cost with consistent accounting; it should not double-subtract the same promotional funding.

No credible ROI point estimate exists from this corpus. A sensitivity table can expose which unknown drives feasibility, but its inputs must be labeled assumptions and tested later.

## 15. The next primary wave

The recommended starting design is two pilot interviews, followed by an initial 8–12 substantive purchase-history interviews, adaptable to access and unresolved contrasts. The team has three people, uncertain recruitment access, and no completed fieldwork in this package. These are practical initial numbers, not evidence of saturation or a representative sample.

Prioritize confirmed 18–30-year-old former Pepsodent Premium users who actually replaced with Basic, then recruit contrasts: retained Premium users, satisfied Basic users, competitor choices, dependent versus independent purchasers, and shared versus personal tubes. Do not use a public profile to validate age or a current tube photograph alone to validate a prior switching sequence.

For each eligible participant, obtain the last two or three purchase/use episodes. The essential facts are product and variant, pack count/grams, who paid and chose, who used it, outlet, approximate paid price and promotion, what ran out, stock at home, alternatives, experienced outcome and subsequent use. Record uncertainty. An interview answer can provide a credible account without becoming a verified transaction or causal effect.

After pilot interviews, revise wording that confuses participants or leads toward the hypothesis. Only then consider a broader survey. A convenience survey can help structure and compare recruited responses; it does not estimate population prevalence merely because it has a large n. A short diary/follow-up for people approaching depletion can reveal later action and reduce reliance on stated intention. Optional exact-SKU shelf checks should be triggered by actual shopping-route accounts.

Each hypothesis needs a prewritten decision rule. For example, if several eligible switchers controlled payment, found the exact SKU, understood its benefit, and still preferred Basic because it was sufficient, weaken a generic education/access story. If a reportedly satisfied user returns to Premium when the desired variant becomes accessible, access becomes more plausible, though selection and counterfactual limits remain. Read the [fieldwork package](../primary_research/) for instruments and blank templates; no respondents or results are invented there.

## 16. How to read the analysis without overstating it

The platform pipelines screen all records computationally and preserve transparent flags. The new close-reading annotation sets contain 109 X records (40 random calibration, 60 theme-screened and nine supplementary purposive), 100 TikTok comments (36 equal-video random calibration and 64 purposive), and 85 Tokopedia reviews (40 seeded random and 45 purposive): 294 annotated records, not 293 people. The Tokopedia analyst also read all 266 main-file texts for screening. The qualitative evidence library is selected to investigate mechanisms and counterexamples. It is not a random estimate of how often each mechanism occurs in Indonesia. Refer to platform sampling and QA files before treating counts as thematic prevalence. Independent assistant checks of quotation fidelity and selected interpretations are quality controls, not human validation or a measured classifier accuracy.

Keep a claim ladder: a visible phrase supports a described account; repeated independent accounts support a plausible mechanism; a confirmed target purchase history supports applicability to that person; a well-designed comparative or experimental design is needed for stronger population/causal conclusions. These steps cannot be skipped with volume.

Five-star ratings can refer to receipt, packaging or expectations before use. New Tokopedia reviews `2127271766` and `2121981403` explicitly report not having tried or not knowing results. Low-rating reviews `845969659`, `2346136191` and `2337020302` concern delivery or packing. Neither extreme can be mapped mechanically to product performance. Reviews containing “cocok,” “promo,” “ngilu” or “ganti” also need context.

Date-quality flags are material. Tokopedia's actor-estimated `reviewTime` can disagree with `reviewDate`; the pilot shows disagreement in 40 of 43 rows. In the full canonical set, 198 reviews have both fields and 186 disagree; 68 have no `reviewTime` and must not be counted as date disagreements. Neither source field has been independently authenticated as the true posting date, so case-window slices remain provisional. TikTok comment date and video date are different, and the case-window comparison uses Asia/Jakarta rather than silently mixing UTC boundaries. A later account may illustrate a mechanism while not explaining the historical case. Current listing titles can change and do not guarantee the older formulation/pack. Unknown country and incentive fields remain unknown, not silently converted to Indonesia and organic.

The existing keyword estimates remain vocabulary evidence, not unique users, willingness to pay, sentiment or market share. The literature pool is purposive; four studies had relevant full-text sections read and 21 were retained at abstract level. Do not treat overlapping reviews of clinical trials as independent clinical confirmations, or transport foreign-category effect sizes into toothpaste forecasts. ## 17. What this work makes possible now

The collection phase has supplied enough material to replace broad guesses with testable explanations, identify obvious weak claims, and design a focused primary wave. It also shows why the team should retain uncertainty: a desirable benefit can lead to continued use, sharing can help specialization, loyal users can seek deals, and Basic can be sufficient. The business problem is unlikely to be resolved by a single undifferentiated story about young people lacking awareness.

Carry forward a short list of decisions: which eligible people have a durable specialist job; who funds and controls replenishment; where the intended choice breaks; what Basic provides; and which proposed change would improve actual appropriate paid replacement at acceptable contribution. Keep the cases that contradict the preferred answer visible until a defensible diagnosis emerges.
