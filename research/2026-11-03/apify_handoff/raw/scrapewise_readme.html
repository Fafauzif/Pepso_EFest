Shopee Reviews Scraper: ratings, comments and seller replies by shop or product

Scrape Shopee reviews of any shop or product in Singapore, Brazil, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, the Philippines, Vietnam and Taiwan into one table: stars, the buyer's comment, product and variation bought, the three detailed scores (product quality, seller service, delivery), the seller's reply, how many photos and videos the buyer attached, likes and date. Filter by stars, keep only reviews with a comment or with photos, and schedule it with Only reviews newer than to pay only for new reviews.

No Shopee account, no cookies, no API key. Works with Shopee's 2026 site, where product reviews are hidden from visitors who are not logged in.

At a glance

  • Price per 1,000 reviews, Free plan: US$ 3.00 (US$ 2.55 on Gold and above)
  • Fee per run: US$ 0.005
  • 8 countries: SG, BR, ID, MY, TH, PH, VN, TW
  • Inputs: shop links, shop ids, shop usernames, product links (/product/<shop>/<item> or ...-i.<shop>.<item>)
  • Depth: up to 5,000 reviews per shop
  • Filters: min and max stars, only with a comment, only with photos or videos, newer than a date or period
  • Shop summary (free): shop rating, total reviews split into good / normal / bad, followers, products, response rate
  • Speed: about 1,000 reviews in 5 to 10 seconds
  • Error rows (invalid link, shop not found, product without reviews in reach): free, with an errorCode

What you can do with it

  • Product research before you source: read the 1 and 2 star reviews of the best sellers in your niche and find what buyers complain about (size, battery, packaging, fake items) before you pick a supplier.
  • Competitor monitoring: schedule a competitor's shop weekly with newerThan: "7 days" and follow their new complaints, scores and how fast they reply.
  • Brand protection: watch your own official shop and your resellers in 8 countries from one Task.
  • Sentiment and AI analysis: feed comments, stars and the three detailed scores to an LLM or a sentiment model; variation tells you which color or size the complaint is about.
  • Seller service audit: sellerReply, sellerReplyAt and ratingSellerService show who answers and how fast.
  • Cross-border vs local sellers: isOverseasSeller and originRegion separate shops shipping from abroad.

The simplest input, one shop:

  "startUrls": ["https://shopee.sg/shop/64922680"],
  "maxReviews": 100

One product, only complaints with a written comment:

How it works, and its limit

Reviews come from the shop's public review page, newest first, up to 5,000 reviews per shop (measured on 2026-10-20). Filtering by stars reaches further back in time.

Products: Shopee does not let visitors open the review list of a single product without logging in. For a product link, the Actor reads its shop's reviews and keeps the ones of that product. For a product in a small or medium shop this covers its reviews of the last weeks or months. For a product in a very large shop (thousands of reviews a day), the 5,000 reviews in reach may cover only a few days, and an older or rarely reviewed product may not show up at all: you then get a free NO_REVIEWS_FOUND row that says how many shop reviews were read. Several products of the same shop in one run share the pages already read.

With includeShopSummary, rows with type: "shop" add shopUsername, shopUrl, shopRating, ratingCount, ratingGood, ratingNormal, ratingBad, followers, productCount, responseRate, isOfficialShop and shopJoinedAt.

Privacy by design: the output never includes the buyer's username, user id, avatar, order id, or links to the photos and videos they uploaded (they may show people). You get the counts only.

FAQ

Do I need a Shopee account or cookies? No.

Why can't I get all reviews of a product? Shopee hides the per-product review list from visitors since 2026. The shop review page is open, and it holds the same reviews mixed across the shop's products, up to 5,000 per shop. See "How it works, and its limit".

Can I get only new reviews every day? Yes. Save a Task with newerThan: "1 day" (or "7 days" for weekly) and schedule it. Reading stops at the first older review, so you only pay for new ones.

Are comments translated? No, they come in the language the buyer wrote (English, Portuguese, Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese, Chinese...).

Is it legal? It reads public review pages that anyone can open without logging in, and leaves out the buyer's identity and uploaded media. How you use the data is your responsibility under Shopee's terms and the privacy laws that apply to you.
