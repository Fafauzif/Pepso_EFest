# Apify feasibility findings

The usable recommendation is to postpone paid collection until existing assistant-collected review evidence has been synthesized. X is the strongest remaining optional source for spontaneous household and purchase-sequence language. Shopee may provide a cross-platform check but should not duplicate a sufficient Lazada corpus solely to increase volume.

Actual collection in this workstream is public GET-only actor metadata, build schemas and documentation. **Zero actors run, zero credit spent.** Twelve actor-input files were generated and validated against archived official schemas; schema validity is not extraction success. Account availability and credit balances remain unverified.

The public API resolved a material price ambiguity. Xquik's Free-tier $0.015 historical fee was superseded on 29 August 2026 by $0.00015, now with separately billed platform usage. The metadata history preserves both. Clockworks' Free fee is $1.25 per 1,000 comments, rather than the $0.50/1,000 headline. Scrapewise Shopee charges $3/1,000 Free reviews plus a start event. No row-count promise follows from these prices.

The supplied Shopee pilot URL is a real independent Bekasi listing, not an official brand store. Product reviews are found through the shop's newest review history, limited to 5,000 reached shop reviews. Busy-store reach may be poor. Exact matching and substantive text must be checked before scaling.

No verified relevant individual TikTok video URL was available from this audit. Therefore no fake or generic example video is included as runnable research input. Brand profiles and unrelated sample videos would not resolve the same question.
