# Exploratory codebook and dictionary

`review_text`: original captured text, untranslated. No grammar repair. `record_id`: platform review ID prefixed with corpus; Reddit is a source-line identifier, not a platform comment ID. `source_url`: public listing/thread/endpoint retrieved. `raw_file` and `raw_locator`/line bounds: trace to captured response. `published_at_utc`: Lazada millisecond timestamp converted to UTC. `within_case_period`: true only for2022-01-01 through2026-06-30 inclusive; no claim of prior historical access. `published_raw`: original relative/date label when precise conversion is unavailable. Do not convert it using the packaging date. `rating`: source supplied rating, not an analyst sentiment label. `displayed_badge`: source badge retained, not independently verified. `self_reported_age`: TryandReview current profile value, not verified age at purchase;0 is source metadata anomaly. `age_verified` and `premium_to_basic_verified`: false throughout. `incentive_status`: explicitly free/campaign, otherwise not established; absence of a badge does not prove no incentive.

Lexical flags are regex searches defined in `scripts/annotate_reviews.py`; a record may have several. They are NOT sentiment, verified motives, or clinical outcomes. `sensory_language` captures taste/freshness/foam/comfort words; `household_language` captures family/chooser-role terms; `price_promotion_language` captures money/discount/shipping-value wording; `pre_use_or_uncertain_result` flags explicit not-yet-used/hoped-for results; `stock_availability_language` flags direct scarcity/channel-friction wording; `repeat_language` includes seller-loyalty wording, so requires manual disambiguation; `sensitivity_language` captures needs/symptom terms; `service_delivery_language` captures shipping/seller/package wording, overlapping actual product reports.

The22 selected manual records are a purposive evidence table. `analyst_mechanism` and `analyst_interpretation_and_boundary` distinguish the proposed mechanism from what the text establishes. `manual_read=true` applies only to those22 selected entries, not the entire corpus. No inter-coder reliability or saturation study occurred.
