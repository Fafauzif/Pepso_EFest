Corporate governance Regulatory announcements Foods combines with McCormick Contacts View Investors Search site Home Brands Current page: Pepsodent Pepsodent Your family’s ultimate oral care protector At Pepsodent, we believe that a healthy mouth, teeth, and gums are essential for a happy life. Backed by cutting-edge research from Unilever scientists, our oral care products provide comprehensive protection and care for your entire mouth. Since launching in 1915, Pepsodent has been setting the benchmark in oral care solutions. Our range of toothpaste and toothbrushes cater to specific oral care needs, offering advanced cavity protection, gum health improvement, and teeth whitening solutions. Pepsodent has always responded to emerging trends in key markets. An example is the Pepsodent Naturals range, which harnesses natural ingredients alongside scientifically backed innovation to deliver fresh breath, anti-cavity protection, and healthy gums. As a global leader in oral disease prevention, Pepsodent brings unmissably superior oral care solutions to consumers worldwide. Preventing oral health disease for all Tooth decay affects a staggering 3.9 billion people globally, and Pepsodent is at the forefront of combatting this by promoting oral health education and accelerating access to dental care. Pepsodent aims to address the lack of access to dental services, which is a prevalent issue in many regions. For example, in Indonesia, more than 8 in 10 people have never visited a dentist due to limited access. This can result in untreated conditions like cavities affecting quality of life and productivity. World Oral Health Day, which is celebrated on 20 March every year, is the largest global awareness campaign on oral health. Pepsodent has been a proud and active supporter for many years, helping to tackle this crisis head-on. Pepsodent’s collaborative efforts with dental professionals and community initiatives help to empower millions to achieve improved dental hygiene. Talk to a dentist Case study For World Oral Health Day, Pepsodent isn’t just marking the occasion; it’s a key moment for the brand to live its purpose. As part of Pepsodent’s mission to make dental care accessible for everyone, the brand launched ‘Now Everyone Can #TalkToADentist’ – a multi-channel campaign offering free teledental consultations to millions, aiming to shatter access barriers to dental care. Pepsodent’s free consultations allow people to connect with dentists using their mobile devices. This service provides professional advice for managing oral health issues at home and guidance on seeking further treatment, if necessary. The campaign reached millions of people globally, creating excitement over these new ‘teledental consultations’. This innovative approach empowers individuals to prioritise their oral health, positioning Pepsodent as a leader in democratising dental care access.
